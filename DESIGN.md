# DORK DESIGN RULES

## Tone
The narrator is dry, cruel, insulting, and completely unfazed by supernatural violence. Horror is played straight; bureaucracy is treated as the normal part.

## Player
Scott is the player, not a pre-scripted personality. Avoid forcing emotions or dialogue into his mouth. The game may insult his choices and reference known biographical details for jokes/puzzles.

## Personalization anchors
- Scott: funny/goofy, confident, state-government executive, law-school graduate but not practicing lawyer, nerd, former D&D player, horror fan, tarot/woo-woo interest.
- Cats: Boo (huge white), Salem (sweet black), Ash (grey scaredy cat), Luna (very scared black cat), Merlin (sweet grey cat).
- Granddaughters: Angela, Julia, Audrey.
- Jorge: huge grotesque government employee treated as completely normal by everyone else.

## World
A secret state-government agency has become / always was an occult dungeon. Scott arrived for an "Executive Continuity Review" and became trapped. The true goal is to escape the building.

## Game structure
- Fixed core map and puzzles so permadeath remains learnable.
- Small optional variations may change between runs later.
- Mastered successful run target: ~1 hour after full expansion.
- One unambiguous true ending.
- No conventional RPG combat. Dangerous encounters are puzzles, environmental problems, social manipulation, or lethal mistakes.

## Difficulty
Difficult but fair. Major lethal outcomes should have clues or warning signs. Knowledge is the main progression system.

## Permadeath
On death, inventory, position, and puzzle state reset. Persistent meta-state may include death log, discovered lore, narrator memory, and completion status. No persistent gameplay buffs or saved puzzle progress.

## Parser philosophy
The parser is a core feature. The player should routinely try a strange action expecting failure and receive a relevant response instead.

Support:
- extensive verb synonym mapping
- natural constructions such as USE X ON Y, GIVE X TO Y, ASK X ABOUT Y
- novelty verbs (lick, smell, kick, pray, threaten, flatter, hide, etc.)
- object-specific responses for important combinations
- varied generic responses for recognized but irrelevant actions
- hidden joke commands and easter eggs

Avoid endless "I don't understand" responses.

## Mobile design
iPhone is a first-class target:
- responsive CRT bezel
- portrait and landscape
- no hover-only interactions
- iOS safe areas
- readable terminal font sizes
- command input always reachable
- large tap targets
- no unnecessary heavy assets or dependencies

## Future expansion
Expand departments into distinct mini-dungeons while preserving one interconnected building. Good candidates: Executive Administration, Records, Legal, Procurement, HR, Occult Compliance, Restricted Archives, Facilities, Finance, IT, Investigations, Communications, and Below.

AI-generated/adaptive content is optional future work and must not be required for the static GitHub Pages release.
